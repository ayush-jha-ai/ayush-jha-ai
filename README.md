### Question 1 · What are you building? `[4 marks]`
 
> Students rarely lose marks because they don't know the content. They lose them because the working on the page doesn't match what the mark scheme is looking for.
>
> **Setura** reads a student's working line by line, maps it to mark-scheme logic, and tells the difference between a **knowledge gap** and a **process error**. It diagnoses. It doesn't tutor.
